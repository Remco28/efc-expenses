EFC trip expenses: review rounds

One page per project, rebuilt after each round. Tap a round to see it. The detail for agents stays in README.md beside this file.

The stakes

A club tool for a handful of parents splitting tournament trip costs. Real money, but small amounts, and nobody uses it for real until early November.

So the review stops when nothing left could lose money or someone's work in normal use. Problems that need a rare timing (two phones in the same second) are worth knowing, not worth endless rounds.

Serious: real use gives a wrong result or loses work, and nobody notices. Medium: needs a rare timing, or you'd see it and can fix it. Counts are after the auditor checked our ratings. Between R11 and the outside review, how costs are split was rebuilt on the club's real rules.

Round 9

Saving on a bad signal: retries that duplicated or dropped corrections, saves that hung, drafts lost on reload. Two serious after the audit, both fixed. This round led to the "hold expenses on the phone until there's a signal" design.

Round 10

A fencer's picked days could vanish when a family day was switched off and on, silently changing coach-cost shares. Fixed, then made moot when attendance days were removed.

Round 11

First review of the rebuilt split. Serious: new fencers started with every day ticked, charging the coach's hotel for days not fenced. You chose "start with none".

Outside review

Codex, fresh. Serious: a slow refresh could send your next action to the wrong trip, and a correction typed during Save's first check was dropped. All six fixed, then put through peers after the fact (they found eight more gaps).

Round 12

No serious problems. Medium: treasurer forms could act on the wrong kind of trip; an edit refused because someone else changed the expense kept failing. Peers broke the first plan (it would have erased the other person's changes) before any code.

Round 13 in one line

No serious problems. Two medium ones, both about a weak signal. The money math and the club's rules check out everywhere.

Medium1 An edit you walk away from mid-save, then refused, is lost
Who it hits
A parent editing an expense who leaves the screen while it's still saving.
How often
Rare: it also needs the save to be refused (someone else changed that expense) or to get no answer.
What they'd see
A brief message that the change couldn't be saved. What they typed is gone; they'd retype it.
Money at risk
None: nothing wrong is saved, a change just doesn't land.
Fix cost
Medium. The planned fix had holes; doing it properly means a protected place to keep the refused edit.
Medium2 A tap that fails with no signal still shows as saved
Who it hits
Someone ticking fencing days (or Fencer) with no signal.
How often
Whenever a tap is made with no signal, which happens at venues.
What they'd see
A "may not have saved" message that fades; the tick stays showing. The treasurer sees the real days on the Fencing days screen before finalizing.
Money at risk
Small: a fencing day that didn't save would shift coach-cost shares until someone notices.
Fix cost
Small for the simple version: a lasting "Not sure this saved, reload to check".

Your call

  1. Keep going: fix both properly (plan, peers, build), then round 14. It will likely find another layer of rare cases.
  2. Stop here (recommended): the simple fix for #2, record #1 as a known rare case, and let a couple of parents try it at the next tournament.
  3. Small question: should the coach's hotel be savable before any fencing days are ticked? Recommended: yes, with a note that it's shared once days are ticked.

Smaller things (low)

Show the 8 low items
  • The Settle up tab's advice for an expense nobody shares says "tick who's sharing", which fits only meals.
  • The in-app family statement still hides a $0.00 share (the PDF was fixed, the screen wasn't).
  • The PDF's "leaves out N expenses" note gets cut off; its family table still says "N going".
  • An old-style expense's form thinks its kind of cost was changed when it wasn't.
  • The Day picker shows "Whole trip" for an expense dated outside the trip.
  • The trip list counts refused expenses as "not sent yet".
  • The PDF prints names in non-Latin scripts as "?"; "12.345" rounds silently.
  • A waiting expense refused after finalizing is seen only on its payer's phone (your earlier choice).

The auditor wants three of these "typing lost on a failed save" items rated medium, not low.

Words used here

Signal
The phone's connection. Saves and reloads need it; "no answer" means the phone couldn't tell whether a save landed.
Refused
The server answered "no", for a reason it gives (the trip was finalized, someone changed the expense first).
Waiting to send
The list of new expenses kept on the phone until there's a signal.
Auditor
A separate agent that checks whether we rated our own problems honestly.
Peers
Fresh agents who try to break a fix plan before any code is written.
