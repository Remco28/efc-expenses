### [MEDIUM] Backspacing a count in Custom split or for an absent family unchecks and disables the field
[public/app.js:957](file:///home/frank/Dev/efc-expenses/public/app.js#L957). Scenario: In an expense with Custom split (where every family starts with 1 share and `autoUnits` is 0), or Per person on a day when a family was absent but ticked to share 1 share, focus the family's unit count input and press Backspace to change "1" to another number (e.g. 2). When the input is temporarily cleared, `u.value === ""` sets `d.split[id] = null`. Because `autoUnits` is 0, line 921 evaluates `away = true` ("Nobody counts" / "Not there that day") and line 922 evaluates `inc = false`. Wrong outcome: The input is instantly re-rendered as `disabled`, the checkbox is unchecked, and input focus is lost mid-edit, blocking the parent from typing their intended count; if saved, the family is excluded from the split. Suggested fix: When `d.basis === "custom"` or `!auto`, keep cleared input at `0` instead of `null`, or do not mark a family `away` when `d.basis === "custom"`.
