# The outside review's six problems, stated plainly

The app is a trip-expense splitter for a fencing club (rules: review/design/billing-model.md). An outside reviewer found and reproduced these six, against commit d4b7f66. Its full report is codex-report.md.

1. **A late refresh can make the next action land on the wrong trip.** On the Fencing days screen, a tap saves and then reloads the trip. If the reload is slow and the parent has already moved to another trip, the old trip's data replaces the one the new screen acts on: volunteering as treasurer on trip B saved it on trip A. (public/app.js: loadTrip and the fencing-days save; the family screen's save is similar.)
2. **A correction typed during Save's first check is lost.** Saving an expense first reloads the trip to see whether anyone joined (a check that can take a while on a weak signal). The form isn't frozen during that check, and what gets saved was taken before it, so a parent's correction typed meanwhile is dropped while the app says "Expense saved". (public/app.js: saveExpense.)
3. **An unfinished meal, reopened after a family was replaced on the same number, charges the new family.** A form being filled in is kept across a reload, naming families by number. Numbers can be reused when the newest family is deleted. The form then shows the replacement family ticked, and saving charges it; the tags sent are looked up from the trip as it is now. (public/app.js: the kept draft and saveExpense.)
4. **Changing someone's name can undo another phone's Fencer change.** The server writes both name and Fencer on every change to a person, taking the field that wasn't sent from what it read a moment before. (src/index.ts: the person update.)
5. **The trip screen can show one treasurer with the fingerprint of another.** The trip's own row is read before the batch that reads everything else and the fingerprint finalize checks, so a treasurer change in between makes a mismatched pair, and finalize accepts it. (src/index.ts: loadTrip.)
6. **A person can be added to a family that just replaced theirs.** Adding a person checks the family's tag, then inserts by number; if the family was deleted and replaced on that number in between, the person lands in the new one. (src/index.ts: adding a person.)
